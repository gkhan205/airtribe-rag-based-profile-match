Finance Manager (FP&A)

We need a Finance Manager to lead budgeting, forecasting and financial analysis for our business units.

Responsibilities
- Own the annual budgeting and monthly forecasting process
- Perform variance analysis and financial modeling for leadership
- Oversee month-end close, financial reporting and GAAP compliance
- Manage treasury and cash flow planning
- Partner with operations on cost reduction and process improvement

Requirements
- Must have: 5+ years of experience
- Must have: Budgeting
- Financial Analysis and forecasting expertise
- Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting; MBA or CPA preferred
- Strong Excel skills; ERP experience (SAP / Oracle) a plus
- Leadership of a team of analysts
