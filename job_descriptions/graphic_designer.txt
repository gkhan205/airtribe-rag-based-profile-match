Graphic Designer

We are hiring a Graphic Designer to create visual assets for marketing campaigns and product.

Responsibilities
- Design branding, logos, print and digital marketing materials
- Produce layouts in Adobe InDesign and illustrations in Adobe Illustrator
- Retouch and compose imagery in Adobe Photoshop
- Create wireframes and prototypes for web pages (UI/UX)
- Maintain typography and brand guidelines

Requirements
- Must have: Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator
- Must have: 2+ years of Graphic Design experience
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Fine Arts or related field
- Portfolio required; 3D modeling or web design a plus
