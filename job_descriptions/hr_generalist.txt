HR Generalist / HR Manager

We are hiring an HR Generalist to manage the full employee lifecycle for a growing company.

Responsibilities
- Own recruiting: job postings, screening, interviewing and offers
- Run onboarding and new hire orientation
- Handle employee relations, investigations and conflict resolution
- Administer benefits, FMLA, 401k and payroll coordination
- Maintain HRIS records and drive performance management cycles
- Ensure compliance with labor law, EEO and FLSA

Requirements
- Must have: Recruiting and Employee Relations experience
- 3+ years of HR experience
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources or Business
- HRIS experience (Workday, ADP, Kronos) preferred
