IT Systems Administrator

We are looking for an IT Systems Administrator to own our on-premise and cloud infrastructure and lead the help desk for a 400-person organization.

Responsibilities
- Administer Windows Server, Active Directory, DNS/DHCP and group policies
- Maintain VMware virtualization hosts and Office 365 / Azure tenants
- Manage LAN/WAN, VPN, firewalls and network security monitoring
- Lead technical support and troubleshooting for hardware and software issues
- Document procedures and drive ITIL-aligned incident management

Requirements
- Must have: 3+ years of IT experience
- Must have: Networking (LAN/WAN, TCP/IP)
- Hands-on Technical Support and Active Directory administration
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or related field
- Nice to have: Linux, ITIL certification, SharePoint, scripting in Python
